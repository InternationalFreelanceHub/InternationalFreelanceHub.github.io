How to Get the Most Out of Outsourcing Digital Work

Outsourcing saves time and money — but only when done right. Here's how to outsource your digital work properly and get amazing results.

📅 May 2025 · 7 min read · By International Freelance Hub
← Back to Blog

The most successful businesses in the world have one thing in common — they don't try to do everything themselves. They outsource tasks to experts, freeing up their time to focus on what they do best. Whether you're a solo entrepreneur, a small business owner, or a content creator, outsourcing your digital work is one of the smartest decisions you can make.

But outsourcing done poorly can lead to wasted money, missed deadlines, and frustrating results. This guide will show you exactly how to outsource your digital work the right way so you always get excellent results.

Why Outsourcing Makes Business Sense

Before we get into the how, let's be clear about the why. Here's what outsourcing gives you:

  • ⏰ Time back — Stop spending hours on tasks outside your expertise
  • 💰 Cost savings — Hiring a freelancer is far cheaper than a full-time employee
  • 🎯 Expert quality — Get work done by specialists who do it every day
  • 📈 Scalability — Scale up or down based on your workload and budget
  • 🌍 Global talent — Access the best talent worldwide, not just locally

Step 1: Know What to Outsource

Not everything should be outsourced. Focus on outsourcing tasks that are:

  • Outside your core skills or expertise
  • Repetitive and time-consuming
  • Technically specialized (SEO, video editing, graphic design)
  • Things you dislike doing but are necessary for your business

Keep in-house the things that require your personal touch — your core business strategy, client relationships, and anything that defines your unique value.

💡 Pro Tip:

Calculate your hourly value. If your time is worth $50/hour and a task takes you 5 hours but you can outsource it for $80, outsourcing saves you $170 of your time. Always think in terms of the value of your time.

Step 2: Define the Task Clearly

The number one reason outsourcing fails is poor communication. Before hiring anyone, document exactly what you need:

  • What — Describe the exact deliverable you expect
  • When — Set a clear deadline with specific dates
  • How — Provide examples, references, or style guides if available
  • Format — Specify file formats, word counts, dimensions, or any technical requirements
  • Quality standard — Share examples of work you consider high quality

Step 3: Choose the Right Service Provider

When choosing who to outsource to, look for these key qualities:

  • ✅ A portfolio of relevant previous work
  • ✅ Clear communication from the start
  • ✅ Realistic timelines — beware of anyone who promises everything too quickly
  • ✅ Positive reviews or testimonials from previous clients
  • ✅ Clear pricing with no hidden costs
⚠️ Red Flags to Watch Out For:

Avoid providers who can't show previous work, are vague about timelines, offer prices that seem too good to be true, or are slow and unclear in their communication from the start.

Step 4: Start Small Before Committing Big

Never give a large project to a new provider without testing them first. Start with a small, low-risk task to evaluate their quality, communication style, and reliability. If they perform well on the small task, you can confidently give them larger projects.

This approach protects your budget and helps you build a reliable team of trusted providers over time.

Step 5: Communicate Throughout the Project

Good outsourcing is not "set and forget." Stay involved throughout the project by:

  • Checking in at agreed milestones
  • Providing feedback promptly when drafts are shared
  • Being available to answer questions quickly
  • Giving clear, specific feedback rather than vague criticism

✅ Do This

  • ✅ Provide detailed briefs
  • ✅ Share reference examples
  • ✅ Respond to questions quickly
  • ✅ Give specific feedback
  • ✅ Pay on time
  • ✅ Build long-term relationships

❌ Avoid This

  • ❌ Vague instructions
  • ❌ Changing requirements mid-project
  • ❌ Ignoring messages for days
  • ❌ Saying "just make it look good"
  • ❌ Delaying payments
  • ❌ Switching providers constantly

Step 6: Build Long-Term Relationships

The best outsourcing relationships are long-term ones. When you find a provider who delivers excellent work, treat them well — pay fairly, communicate respectfully, and give them repeat business. Over time, they'll understand your brand deeply and deliver better results with less briefing needed.

Think of your outsourcing partners as an extension of your team, not just a one-time transaction. The businesses that build strong, loyal outsourcing relationships consistently get better results and better rates than those who constantly switch providers.
